The Hyperverse began with nothing except the Timestream. At an unknown moment, an infinite paradox exploded into existence, giving birth to the Hyperverse itself: a cosmic structure containing countless multiverses and independent universes. Every fictional world exists somewhere within it, from Marvel and DC to Disney, fantasy worlds, science fiction settings, and countless original realities. Inter-universal travel eventually became possible, allowing civilizations, heroes, villains, governments, and ordinary people to move freely between universes. Some universes remained completely neutral, belonging to no civilization and serving as common ground.

As contact between universes increased, so did conflict. To quietly guide history away from disaster, five of the wisest beings in existence formed a secret council known as the Five Elders. Rather than ruling openly, they observed the Hyperverse from the shadows, intervening only when they believed events threatened the stability of reality itself. Despite their efforts, warfare continued to escalate. Some conflicts remained confined within a single universe, while others spilled across dimensional borders, dragging entire civilizations into wars that affected multiple universes simultaneously.

Eventually the situation became unsustainable. The Five Elders stepped into the open and founded the First Celestial Government, bringing together respected figures from across the Hyperverse to create the first inter-universal authority. Travel between universes became heavily regulated. Machines capable of opening unrestricted dimensional passages were outlawed, confiscated, and destroyed, with official travel now managed through government oversight. Large-scale inter-universal wars gradually disappeared, ushering in the first era of relative stability.

That peace ended when one of the Five Elders fell under the influence of an ancient cosmic entity known as the Unfinished Engimythica. Corrupted from within, the Elder betrayed the council, triggering a devastating civil war among the founders of the new order. Although the remaining Elders eventually prevailed, the betrayal fundamentally changed their outlook. The lesson they took from the conflict was not that openness had failed, but that trust itself had become a liability.

In response, the First Celestial Government transformed into the Second Celestial Empire. The surviving Five Elders became the Four Emperors after the death of their corrupted companion. Fearful that another existential threat could emerge without warning, the Empire centralized power and began developing Catasthor, a colossal superweapon capable of severing an entire universe from the Hyperverse, permanently isolating it from all inter-universal travel and contact.

The Empire’s paranoia continued to grow. Citizens, organizations, and influential individuals were arrested simply because they were considered potential threats. Preventive imprisonment became common, and entire universes lived under constant surveillance. These increasingly authoritarian policies convinced many that resistance was no longer optional. Rebel movements appeared throughout the Hyperverse, and many began constructing their own advanced weaponry, including extremely powerful fission weapons intended to counter the Empire’s overwhelming military advantage.

Seeking to eliminate resistance without fighting endless campaigns, the Empire launched Operation Null Coercion. Enormous bounties, paid in Celestial Credits (CC), were placed on the heads of prominent rebel leaders. This policy created an explosion in professional bounty hunting. Earth-Alpha, already a lawless world, became the center of the bounty hunting economy, attracting mercenaries, criminals, information brokers, smugglers, and assassins from across the Hyperverse.

Recognizing that scattered resistance could never defeat the Empire, the major rebel organizations united into independent governments. Three major powers emerged: the Aleph Authority, the Independent State of Universes (ISU), and the Free Celestial Republic. Rather than operating as isolated rebel cells, they became functioning governments with their own territories, militaries, economies, and political leadership. The Second Celestial Empire viewed these governments as direct competitors and refused to acknowledge their legitimacy.

Expecting total war, the three governments began a joint military research initiative known as Project Parabellum. Their scientists, engineers, and military researchers collaborated on the development of increasingly sophisticated fissure weaponry capable of matching the Empire’s superweapons. When intelligence regarding these projects reached the Four Emperors, the Empire issued an ultimatum demanding that all fissure weapon development immediately cease. Failure to comply would result in war.

The ultimatum was ignored.

The resulting conflict became the largest war in Hyperverse history. Entire universes became battlefields. The rebel governments deployed their newly developed fission weapons while the Empire responded by activating Catasthor. Multiple universes were permanently sealed away from the Hyperverse, while countless fleets, armies, and civilizations were destroyed. Although the rebels fought fiercely, they suffered catastrophic losses as the Empire’s superior infrastructure and strategic position gradually overwhelmed them.

Realizing that complete military defeat had become inevitable, the leaders of the rebel governments agreed to peace negotiations with the Four Emperors. The negotiations were, in reality, a final act of sacrifice. During the summit, the rebel leadership detonated concealed devices that killed themselves alongside all Four Emperors. Neither side emerged victorious. The war simply lost its leadership overnight.

With both governments effectively decapitated, survivors from every side began rebuilding the political order. The authoritarian Empire was dismantled and replaced by the Third Celestial Democratic Republic (TCDR). Unlike its predecessors, the TCDR was governed by a parliament composed of representatives from across the Hyperverse rather than by a small group of rulers. New laws established regulated inter-universal travel, strict oversight of advanced weapons research, and economic reconstruction. Celestial Credits remained the standard currency, while large recovery funds were distributed to repair the enormous damage left by the war.

Although the new republic brought greater stability, complete peace never returned. Small inter-universal wars and border conflicts continued to occur. Criminal organizations flourished in places beyond effective government control, especially on Earth-Alpha, which remained the Hyperverse’s criminal capital. Catasthor itself was preserved rather than destroyed and converted into a museum documenting one of the darkest periods in Hyperverse history. Rumors nevertheless persisted that the weapon remained operational, while conspiracy theories claimed the Republic secretly maintained hidden stockpiles of prohibited fission weapons.

Years later, another discovery shocked the Republic. Explorers located a forgotten planet containing Project Voidstar, the Second Celestial Empire’s unfinished second superweapon. Officially known as the False Vacuum Decay Cannon, the weapon was only partially complete but already occupied much of the planet’s surface. Construction equipment, manufacturing infrastructure, engineering records, and enormous blueprint archives remained intact. Among the recovered documents were preliminary plans for two even more ambitious superweapons, Project Omega and Project Excalibur, both abandoned before construction began. Like Catasthor, Project Voidstar was preserved as a museum rather than dismantled.

Unknown to the TCDR, another danger had been quietly growing throughout these years. Deep within abandoned facilities left behind by the Second Celestial Empire, Dr. Breen assembled a new power known as the Combine Empire. Salvaging forgotten military infrastructure, abandoned factories, and discarded imperial technologies, the Combine secretly rebuilt an industrial and military machine on an enormous scale. Entire armies were trained, new technologies were developed, and the remains of the old Empire became the foundation for something even more dangerous.

Rather than attacking immediately, Dr. Breen pursued political infiltration. Combine sympathizers established a parliamentary faction inside the TCDR called the Harmonious Development Coalition. Publicly they promoted stability, efficiency, and stronger central government. Behind the scenes they worked entirely for the Combine Empire. Over time they gained enough seats and influence to shape legislation, weaken the Republic’s institutions, delay investigations, and quietly dismantle the government’s ability to respond to external threats.

By the time the Republic realized the extent of the infiltration, it was already too late. The Combine’s political agents had hollowed out the TCDR from within. Security systems had been compromised, military readiness reduced, and countless officials had unknowingly advanced the interests of the enemy.

Only then did Dr. Breen reveal the Combine Empire.

Using the Republic’s own political paralysis against it, Combine fleets emerged from hidden shipyards throughout the Hyperverse and began a coordinated campaign of conquest. Simultaneous military offensives struck across multiple universes while Combine supporters inside the TCDR sabotaged communications, logistics, intelligence, and government response. What had begun decades earlier as secret reconstruction inside abandoned imperial facilities became a full-scale invasion. The Hyperverse once again entered an age of total war, this time facing an enemy that had spent years weakening it from the inside before ever firing the first shot.
