01 / The standard

No mystery.
Just the grade.

SGS evaluates the four places condition reveals itself: centering, corners, edges and surface. Each result is visible on the label, paired with a unique certificate and sealed inside a crystal-clear presentation case.

Transparent

Four subgrades make the final result easy to read, compare and trust.

Traceable

A unique certificate number ties the card, label and grade together.

Protected

A fitted inner well and tamper-evident shell keep the card presentation-ready.
